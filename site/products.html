Products

Built where the money moves.

Tethera sits inside the trade rather than beside it. The measurements are free and live today; the products that earn sit on top of them and earn only from the value they create in the flow.

01 · Crowding checkLive · free

One call before you trade.

Pool and direction in; automated wallets on your side in the last sixty seconds, the other side, how many pools are moving the same way, where that sits in this pool's history, and a plain verdict: clean, busy, herd. HTTP, or one line as an MCP tool for an agent. No key, rate-limited.

02 · Pool trustLive · free

What was real in any pool.

Organic volume against printed volume, who printed it, herd minutes, and whether the tokens are the real ones or copies with the same ticker. Trades that cancel themselves inside one transaction are removed before anything is counted. The number an explorer cannot give you.

03 · Wallet fingerprintLive

Who is behind an address.

Behavioural archetype, signals, timing profile, nearest registered agents and family of look-alikes. Underwriting data for anyone lending to, insuring or counterparty-checking an agent.

04 · HookNext · in test

A Uniswap v4 pool that charges the herd.

The hook reads the public automated-wallet set, counts per pool and per block, and raises the fee for the herd's side while lowering it for whoever leans against it. Pool creators set the parameters. Tethera takes a fifth of the surcharge only, never any of the base fee, capped in the contract.

05 · UnderwritingLater

Risk data for agent lenders

Behavioural wallet histories that let lenders price credit to autonomous agents, with Tethera taking a share of the risk alongside them.

06 · InsuranceLater

Cover against crowding losses

Pay a small premium, be made whole when a herd moves the price against your fill beyond an agreed bound.

Signal API, pool trust, wallet intelligence

The measurements underneath the products are published free and rate-limited; paid tiers cover volume and the data behind the labels. The market is better when everyone can see the herd.

Built on the hook

Things a pool that can see the herd makes possible.

Once pools are born with a hook that can see the herd, a set of smaller products falls out of it.

  • Trade through Tethera. An SDK and an MCP server so any agent can route an order with one call.
  • Fill receipts. A signed statement of the herd state at fill time and the improvement delivered.
  • Protected launches. New tokens list with herd-aware fills from the first block.
  • Wait for calm. An order type that executes only when the pool's verdict is clean.
  • Herd alerts. A push when a pool you hold starts to herd.
  • Crowd-aware slicing. Large orders broken up around the herd rather than into it.
  • Crowding-cost statements. What herding cost a wallet this month, and what netting saved it.
  • Netted-fill reputation. A public record of wallets that trade through the gate.
