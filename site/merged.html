How it works

See the herd. Charge it. Prove it.

See the herd

Every swap on the chain is labelled by behaviour within seconds of landing: several pools in one block, sub-second cadence, computed sizes, fast round trips, reaction to other wallets. Seven signals over seven days, one label. The agent registry is the test set, not the label.

Charge the herd, pay the other side

A Uniswap v4 hook reads the public set of automated wallets and counts them per pool, per block. When a herd forms, the pool's fee rises for the herd's side and falls for whoever leans against it. Liquidity providers stop paying for the busiest minutes, and the herd pays for its own impact.

Prove it

Herd minutes, the price move that follows them, the fees collected and the trades removed as self-cancelling are published every week, with the pools named. Anyone with a node can repeat the count.
