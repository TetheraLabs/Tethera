How these are counted

A wallet is automated when at least three independent behavioural signals fire across five or more swaps in the last seven days: trades in several pools inside one block, sub-second gaps between trades, metronomic timing, round trips completed within seconds, sizes that are computed rather than chosen, reaction to a price move within one block, and persistence over days. The ERC-8004 agent registry is used to check the labels, not to make them: registered agents barely trade, so a registry alone would miss almost every bot on the chain.

A busy minute has fifteen or more automated wallets in one pool. A herd is a busy minute where four in five of them are on the same side. Numbers are recomputed every ten minutes and the weekly report fixes them for the record.
