Tethera

tokenised equities · robinhood chain

A research desk whose record you can check.

Tethera searches for trading strategies in tokenised stocks and ETFs, scores them under a locked rule on years chosen before the search, validates them on years the search never saw, and keeps a hash-chained record of every trial, every improvement and every read of sealed data. The strategies are private. The protocol, the price construction and the record are public.
