How it works

See the herd. Net it. Prove the saving.

See the herd

Every swap on the chain is labelled by behaviour within a second of landing. Same-block multi-pool trades, sub-second cadence, computed sizes. No registry needed; the registry is the test set, not the label.

Net it

When many wallets want the same trade in the same minute, Tethera matches opposing orders first and settles the rest at one uniform price, as a filler on UniswapX. Nobody signs up. The reactor awards the fill to the best price.

Prove the saving

Every fill comes back with the basis points saved against the pool, checkable on-chain. The fee is a share of that number, so it's only paid when the saving happened.
