Shepherd's Warning

A free weekly report on where the herd ran.

Red sky in the morning. Every week we publish where the herds formed on Robinhood Chain, how long they lasted, and what they cost.

What's in an issue

  • The ten most crowded minutes of the week, with the pools and sides.
  • Which pools herded most, and which stayed calm all week.
  • How long herds lasted, and how many persisted past a minute.
  • An estimate of what herding cost the agents inside it.
  • One thing we learned about how agents behave on the chain.

Plain language, real numbers, no sign-up wall. The first issue goes out the week of 21 October 2026. Past issues will be listed here.
