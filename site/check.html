Check a token

Was it real?

Paste a token contract on Robinhood Chain. We count every swap in its pools over the last seven days and say who was there: people, software, wallets born for the occasion, or one wallet trading with itself.

Takes up to half a minute the first time a token is checked.

Shares are of organic swaps or wallets, self-cancelling trades removed. "Fresh" and "born together" are shown only for tokens whose first trade falls inside the window. The rules are on the Method page; the raw answer is at .
