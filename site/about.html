About

Yan, tan, tethera.

One, two, three. Shepherds in Cumbria counted their flocks in a tongue older than English, and a count that still runs on the fells gave this project its name.

What Tethera is

Tethera Labs builds market infrastructure for autonomous trading agents, starting on Robinhood Chain. The first products are live and free: a crowding check that tells any agent, in one call, how crowded its next trade is; a trust profile for any pool that separates real volume from printed; and a behavioural fingerprint for any wallet. The first on-chain product is a Uniswap v4 hook that charges the herd and pays whoever leans against it. Behind all of it sits a live map of every automated wallet on the chain.

What we believe

Agents will do most of the trading on-chain within a few years. They will be good at reading prices and bad at reading each other, because nothing on the chain tells them who else is about to act. That gap is a cost today and a risk tomorrow. We think the fix is a shared view of the herd, published openly, and execution that uses it.

The data is free because the market is better when everyone can see the herd. The products earn only when they make a trade better than it would have been.
